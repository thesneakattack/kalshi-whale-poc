Treat the newly observed issue as a debugging task inside the Quality Control Plane initiative.

Do not patch the symptom first.

Establish:
1. observed failure,
2. reliable reproduction,
3. competing plausible hypotheses,
4. least-invasive measurement that distinguishes them,
5. measurement result,
6. confirmed root cause,
7. why existing tests/diagnostics/CI did not detect it,
8. smallest correct fix,
9. regression test,
10. permanent detection disposition:
   - runtime diagnostic,
   - CI guard,
   - both/shared logic,
   - existing guard,
   - genuinely one-off with explanation.

Measure before optimizing or changing behavior.

Keep unrelated cleanup out of the bugfix commit.

After fixing, rerun the affected task's verification and report the evidence.
